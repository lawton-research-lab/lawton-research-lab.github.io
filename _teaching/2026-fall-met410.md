Primary instructor for a 4-credit stacked undergraduate/graduate course (MET 410/510) introducing the fundamental equations governing synoptic-scale atmospheric motion, derived from the conservation of momentum, mass, and energy. Students simplify these equations in isobaric coordinates to relate wind, pressure, and temperature fields as used in weather analysis and forecasting, connecting the mathematics to physical intuition about the atmosphere. Topics include advection, kinematic flow, forces, scale analysis, and thermal wind. Also supervise the teaching assistant who leads the course's laboratory section.
